# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 2024**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mitchell Gordon ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you subscribe to SMS notifications on mitchgordon.me.

## Information We Collect

When you subscribe to our SMS notification service, we collect:
- Your phone number

## How We Use Your Information

We use your phone number solely to send you SMS notifications about new blog posts published on mitchgordon.me.

## Information Sharing

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.**

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your phone number or any mobile information to outside parties.

## Data Retention

We retain your phone number only as long as you remain subscribed to our SMS service. Upon opting out, your information will be deleted.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP
- Request deletion of your phone number by contacting us

## Opt-Out

To stop receiving SMS messages, reply STOP to any message. You may also use: CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, OPTOUT, or STOPALL.
